# Food52 Site Map Analysis

## Website Analyzed

Food52 ([food52.com](https://food52.com/)) is a food and recipe website with an editorial magazine style.

## Information Architecture

Food52 organizes recipes alongside articles, videos, collections, and shopping content. Recipes are grouped into broad categories and then narrower subcategories.

## Site Map

- Home
  - Featured recipes
  - Seasonal collections
  - Editorial stories
- Recipes
  - Dinner
    - Chicken
    - Pasta
    - Vegetarian
  - Dessert
    - Cake
    - Chocolate
    - Cookies
  - Breakfast
  - Drinks
- Collections
  - Seasonal recipes
  - Weeknight recipes
  - Baking collections
- Articles
- Videos
- Shop
- About

## Observation

Broad categories help visitors begin browsing, while subcategories make the recipe archive easier to scan. Collections and articles provide additional ways to discover recipes.
