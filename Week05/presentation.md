# Five-Minute Presentation Outline

Use these notes as prompts and explain the project in your own words.

## 0:00-0:45 - Concept and audience

My proposed project is Weekend Oven, a small baking journal for beginners.
It grows out of the chocolate brownie recipe I worked on in Weeks 1-3.
The audience is someone who wants to bake something to share on a weekend
and needs a clear recipe without unrelated distractions.

The main visitor task is to choose a recipe, check the ingredients, and follow
the method. I want the website to feel like a small food magazine while still
being practical to use in a kitchen.

## 0:45-1:30 - Inspiration

Show the Food52 reference linked in proposal.md.

My inspiration is Food52's connection between food writing and recipes.
I want a short editorial introduction, clear typography, and visible food
photography. My version will be much smaller, with my own layout and content.
My proposed palette is white and charcoal with cherry-red links and sage labels.

## 1:30-2:30 - Sitemap and wireframes

Open site-map.md and wireframes.png.

The site has five pages: Home, Recipes, Chocolate Brownies, Lemon Cookies,
and About. Home features the brownie recipe. The recipe index links to both
recipes, and every page keeps the same navigation.

The wireframe shows desktop and phone versions. Home has two recipe previews
side by side on desktop and stacked on mobile. A recipe page has ingredients
beside the method on desktop and above it on mobile. The photograph rectangles
are placeholders; I still need to prepare the second recipe's content and image.

## 2:30-3:30 - Current skills

Open ../Week03/index.html or its source as an example.

My current brownie page already uses semantic HTML, local images with alt text,
ingredient and method lists, and links to page sections. Its external CSS uses
padding, margins, borders, and flexbox for navigation. These give me a starting
point for the shared page structure.

For the new project I will practice positioning for the featured-photo title.
I will keep the navigation simple because five pages do not require a dropdown.

## 3:30-4:30 - Skills to learn and scope

I still need to understand media queries well enough to choose my own
breakpoints, keep image crops consistent, and check keyboard focus and contrast.
I also need to practice positioning so the photo title stays readable.

The core project will work with HTML and CSS. It does not include accounts,
shopping, or a search engine. That keeps the scope realistic for the midterm.

After approval, I will prepare the remaining content, build the shared HTML
structure, style the layouts, and check the pages on a phone-sized screen.

I would like feedback on whether two recipes provide enough content for these
