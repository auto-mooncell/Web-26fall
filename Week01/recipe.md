# Chocolate Brownies

## About This Recipe

These **chocolate brownies** are rich, fudgy, and easy to make. They are a good dessert for sharing with friends.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
2. Mix the melted butter and sugar in a large bowl.
3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, then mix until smooth.
4. Stir in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
6. Pour the batter into a lined baking pan.
7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes.
8. Let the brownies cool before cutting them into squares.

## Baking Note

For **fudgier brownies**, do not overbake them. Let them cool completely before serving.
