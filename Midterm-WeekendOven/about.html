Weekend Oven started with a chocolate brownie recipe. Butter, sugar, cocoa, and a few quiet minutes in the kitchen: nothing elaborate, just something worth making.

This journal is for beginners, occasional bakers, and anyone who likes bringing a homemade treat to the table. You don't need a perfect kitchen or a cupboard full of special equipment.

Start small. Read the recipe. Enjoy the part where your kitchen begins to smell like chocolate.

A few kitchen notes

01

Before you begin

Read all the steps before you start. Measure your ingredients, line the pan, and preheat the oven. It is much easier to bake when you are not looking for the flour halfway through mixing.

A kitchen scale is useful when a recipe gives weights. For this cup-based brownie recipe, spoon flour into the measuring cup and level the top instead of packing it down.

02

The cooling pause

Warm brownies are delicate. Leave them in the pan until they have cooled completely, then lift them out using the parchment overhang.

For tidy squares, use a sharp knife and wipe it between cuts. If the center still feels soft, give it a little more cooling time.
